RVA's Chess Club

RVA Pieces, The Richmond Chess Club, a 501(c)3 nonprofit organization, exists to foster a thriving environment and active community for casual and competitive chess in our city, as well as provide access to stellar chess education for everyone, regardless of age or skill level.

View Upcoming Events Get In Touch

Casual Games

You can catch us for casual games on multiple days of the week. Come say hi! All are welcome regardless of age or level of experience.

Tournaments

Every other week, we host the next round of our official USCF rated ladder. When the weather is warm, we meet monthly to compete for bragging rights at Backyard Blitz.

Beginner Meetups

If you’re new to chess or have recently come back to it, you can meet other beginners at our Beginners Only meetups. Starting November, we’ll be relaunching our free beginner classes.
